Research

My research interests focus on generative video intelligence, including video generation, long video generation, video world models, distillation, and memory mechanisms.

01

Video Generation

High-quality visual synthesis, controllable generation, and temporal consistency for dynamic video content.

02

Long Video Generation

Long-horizon generation with coherent motion, scene continuity, and scalable temporal modeling.

03

Video World Models

Learning predictive video representations for physical dynamics, action-conditioned simulation, and world-aware generation.

04

Distillation

Compressing and transferring generative capabilities for efficient video models and deployable inference.

05

Memory

Memory mechanisms for long-context video generation, identity persistence, and retrieval-augmented visual modeling.
